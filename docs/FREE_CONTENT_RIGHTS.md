# Free commercial-use content: starter rights register

Reviewed: 2026-09-23. Scope: four text-only learning cards, not clearance of the existing feed.

No paid contract, publisher partnership, or exclusive rights have been obtained. These are existing public licenses, usable only subject to their conditions. Attribution snapshots are stored with the four licensed cards and validated/backfilled for older device saves. Snapshots identify the card, original work, creator, license, modifications, and review date; they are not grants to import other content. RSS ingestion withholds publisher excerpts and images, including old RSS saves at render time. All 765 unverified catalog excerpt fields have also been removed from the shipped catalog; their originals remain in Git history before this change. All 1,000 catalog card IDs, links, and other fields are preserved. Closed-by-default checks also cover catalog cards, collapsed previews, repository listings, and search. Other quotation and artwork rights remain outside this implementation. Deployment status is reported separately.

## Attribution for integration

For an adapted card based on Web Vitals, include visible linked credit equivalent to:

“Adapted from Web Vitals by Philip Walton / Google (source link), under CC BY 4.0 (license link). Condensed and rewritten by StackScroll.”

For an adapted DevTools card, include equivalent credit naming Google and the original page. Use an accurate change description; do not label unchanged excerpts as rewritten. Retain supplied copyright, license, and disclaimer notices as applicable. Do not suggest endorsement. Include a route to Google's site policy and original source. Do not impose terms or technical restrictions that prevent recipients exercising the licensed rights in the reused material.

Attribution is required in the actual published experience, not solely in this internal register. Saving a card should retain its source and license metadata. A future commercial model must distinguish StackScroll's proprietary features from the reusable licensed material.

## Admission policy for the first free-content batch

1. Prefer CC0/public-domain material with verified provenance or CC BY 4.0 prose. Confirm each work's scope and exceptions.
2. Record exact work URL, creator, publisher, license/version/link, evidence URL, review date, permitted media, and actual modifications.
3. Review images independently. Never infer image permission from an RSS URL or a text license. No image is cleared by this register.
4. Exclude NonCommercial content from this commercial-use batch. Defer NoDerivatives and ShareAlike material pending a specific compliance approach; ShareAlike can permit commercial use but has additional obligations.
5. Do not expand permissions to the entire publisher feed. New articles require evidence before approval.
6. Keep unknown rights pending. A source link, availability check, short excerpt, or private deployment is not permission.
