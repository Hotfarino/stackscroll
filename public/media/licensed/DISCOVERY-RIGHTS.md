# Discovery image rights

Reviewed September 23, 2026. Sixteen individually reviewed works accompany original, image-specific lessons. These are reference/archive images, not fresh news or implied partnerships.

Every displayed image has a creator credit, source link, license link and change notice. BY-SA adaptations remain under their original BY-SA license; those image terms do not apply to StackScroll source code. Public-domain NASA/EPA works are used factually, not as product endorsements. No NC or ND licensed works are included.

See DISCOVERY-RIGHTS.json for exact-work grants, original URLs, editorial caveats and transformations. Images are local WebP files, at most 1200px, without enlargement. Braille is cropped to the tactile-cell row. Full-frame presentation preserves scientific comparisons and narrow/portrait subjects. No generic fallback is attached to unrelated articles.

Excluded: NOAA glider (weak composition and subject-id discrepancy), thermal cameras with prominent marks, and softer printer alternatives. Battery chemistry is deliberately unspecified because the source descriptions conflict.
